# Pricing — Mycro

Mycro is grow-operations software for small gourmet & functional mushroom farms (oyster, lion's mane, shiitake and similar culinary/medicinal species). It runs in any web browser — nothing to install. The free tools are live today, and so are sign-in accounts, which a one-time Founding Grower spot opens. The monthly Grower/Farm plans are not open yet.

All prices in USD. Secure checkout by Stripe. Monthly-plan prices are targets and may change; the $99 Founding Grower price is live and locks whatever those rates become, for life. Last updated: 2026-08-02.

## Free — $0
- Price: **$0 / month**, no account required.
- The free **Grow Log** (batch tracker) at https://usemycro.com/grow-log.html — log batches from inoculation → colonization → fruiting → harvest, with automatic biological efficiency, days-in-stage, overdue-colonization flag, and contamination rate. Saves in the browser; exports CSV/JSON. No item cap.
- The **Grow Calculator**, **Substrate Recipe Calculator**, and **Fruiting Conditions** cheat-sheet (14 species).
- All grower guides.
- Automatic backup: the log syncs itself to a private link you can open on any device, free, with no account.
- Cloud free tier (when the monthly plans open): up to **3 active batches** with an account.

## Grower — ~$12/month (early-access target) · most popular
- Price: **~$12 / month**.
- Everything in Free, plus:
- **Unlimited** active batches.
- Full grow log on a signed-in account across devices, with multi-season history.
- Contamination analytics, yield & biological-efficiency (BE) reports.
- COGS & pricing, harvest calendar, CSV export.

## Farm — ~$24/month (early-access target)
- Price: **~$24 / month**.
- Everything in Grower, plus:
- Wholesale order management.
- Pack lists & labels.
- Multi-user (whole-team access).
- Simple invoicing.
