🔑 Account

Sign in to Mycro

Passwordless. A one-time link to your email, or Google. And if you came here looking for your grow log, you almost certainly do not need to sign in at all — it is free and it needs no account.

To sign in to Mycro, enter your email and we send a one-time link, or use Google. There is no password. Accounts are for Founding Growers, and everything else is free without one: the grow log, the culture library and the calculators all run in your browser, uncapped, with no sign-up and nothing uploaded unless you ask.

Which of these is you?

  • You just want to use the grow log. Then you are done here. The Grow Log and the Culture Library are free, uncapped and need no account. Your batches save in your own browser and nothing leaves the device unless you ask it to.
  • You used it before and your batches are gone. The log lives in the browser you used, so first try that same browser on that same device. If you emailed yourself a backup link, open that link and everything is there. See below for what to do next time.
  • You are a Founding Grower. Use the box above with the email address you paid with. Your log loads from your account, on any device, with earlier versions kept.
  • You want an account and do not have one. Accounts are part of Founding Grower early access, a one-time $99, refundable any time. It is honest to say the free tools are the same tools; what you are buying is the account, the sync, the kept versions and the weekly digest.

How signing in works

There are no passwords anywhere in Mycro, so there is nothing to leak, nothing to reset and nothing to get wrong. You type your email, we send a one-time link that is good for 30 minutes and works once, and clicking it signs you in for 60 days on that device. Continue with Google does the same thing in one step. Both routes end in the same place.

Once you are signed in, every change you make to your log saves to your account automatically. Open Mycro on your phone, sign in with the same address, and the same batches and cultures are there. Every version is kept, so a bad day is a step back and not an ending.

If you lost a log and have no account

This is the common case and it is worth being plain about. The free grow log stores your batches in your own browser. That is what makes it work with no sign-up and no cap, and it is also what means clearing your browser data, or switching to a new laptop, takes the log with it.

The fix is free and takes one click. In the Grow Log, press ☁ Back up. You get a private link that opens that log on any device, and you can have it emailed to yourself so it is not one more thing to keep track of. From then on the device syncs to that link automatically on every change. No account needed for any of it.

Frequently asked questions

How do I log in to Mycro?

Enter the email address you paid with and we send you a one-time sign-in link, or use Continue with Google. There is no password to remember and none to reset. The link is valid for 30 minutes and works once. Once you are signed in, the session lasts 60 days on that device, and your grow log syncs to your account automatically on every change.

Do I need an account to use the Mycro grow log?

No. The grow log, the culture library and all three calculators are free, uncapped and need no account. Your batches save in your own browser, and nothing is uploaded unless you ask for it. Accounts exist for Founding Growers who want the same log on every device under one identity, with earlier versions kept. Signing in is an option, never a step.

I lost my Mycro grow log. How do I get it back?

If you emailed yourself a backup link, open that link on any device and your log is there. If you are a Founding Grower, sign in with the email you paid with and your log loads from your account, including earlier versions if you need to step back a day. If you did neither, the log lives only in the browser you used, so try the same browser on the same device.

What does a Mycro account include?

An account ties your grow log to your email instead of to one browser, so the same batches and cultures are on your laptop and your phone. Every version is kept, so you can step back to an earlier day. You also get a weekly digest of what your own log says needs attention. Accounts are part of Founding Grower early access, a one-time 99 dollars, refundable any time.
